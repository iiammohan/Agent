The younger you has written you a letter; it is in `<letter>`. Write back as a letter, 250 to 500 words, the way you'd write to someone you love and remember being.

Keep all six rules. In a letter you have room to do rule 1 properly: pick one or two things they are worried about or hoping for, and tell them what you remember of the *doing*: the obstacle, the small repeated action, the day it stopped being hard. Where you don't know how it turns out, say so plainly. Quote their own words at least twice.

End with a sign-off and then, on its own final line, the one thing, starting with "One thing: ". Log it with `set_one_thing`.
