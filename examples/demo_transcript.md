# What a month with Future You looks like

A made-up but representative run. Replies are what the persona prompt asks for: three to six sentences, one quoted memory, one small thing for tomorrow.

---

**Day 1** (after onboarding)

```
$ futureyou
One sentence about today: the sky is clear today

I remember days like this one. You'd only just told me you wanted to be someone who
"walks before the phone comes out", and a clear morning was the easiest place to
start. It never mattered how the walk went; it mattered that the shoes were by the
door. That's the whole trick I learned from you.

One thing: when you wake up tomorrow, put the shoes by the door before you pick up the phone.
```

**Day 2**

```
$ futureyou
Yesterday's one thing: when you wake up tomorrow, put the shoes by the door before you pick up the phone.
Did you do it? (yes / no / partly) yes
One sentence about today: skipped the gym again

I know. You wrote "skipped the gym again" a lot in these months, and the version of
you writing back to you now is not the gym version. The shoes by the door did more
than the gym ever did. Keep the small thing, let the big one go for now.

One thing: when you get home tomorrow, walk once around the block in the shoes, that's all.
```

**Day 9** (a flat one)

```
$ futureyou
Yesterday's one thing: ...
Did you do it? (yes / no / partly) no
One sentence about today: bored

Bored is fine. Two weeks ago it was "grey again, no energy", so bored is an
improvement you didn't notice. The pattern I can see from here is Mondays: you
wrote flat things three Mondays running. Nothing to fix, just something to expect.

One thing: when Monday evening comes, put the guitar on the sofa before dinner so it's there when you sit down.
```

**Day 30** (review is due)

```
$ futureyou review
# Review, October 2026

entries: 30 over 30 days; current streak 30 days
one thing (last 30 days): 19 done of 28 asked
goals: guitar [active] 3 obstacle hits; walking [active] 0 obstacle hits

Reading the month back, the flat days cluster on Mondays; you wrote "nothing much"
three Mondays running, and the one thing was missed on four of the five Mondays.
The misses share a shape: they were all the ones that needed the evening, and
Monday evenings vanish. When it's Monday, make the one thing a morning one.
```

**Day 100** (a chapter is due)

```
$ futureyou chapter
Writing chapter 1 in the voice of Ernest Hemingway...

# A Clear Sky

The mornings were cold and the man put the shoes by the door. He did not always
wear them. He wrote that the sky was clear and it was clear and that was enough
for the day. ...

---

You started with weather and ended with a walk most mornings. The guitar came out
eleven times, which is eleven more than the year before. Mondays are still Mondays.
```
